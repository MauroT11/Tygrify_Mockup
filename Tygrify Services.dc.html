What we do not do

So you are not left guessing: we do not offer paintless dent removal, chassis or jig straightening, classic restoration, ADAS recalibration, EV and hybrid repair, diagnostics, MOTs, servicing, wheel alignment, air conditioning, glass and windscreen work or vehicle wrapping. Recovery is arranged through a third party. There is no courtesy car.

If you are not sure whether a job is one of ours, send us a photo and we will tell you either way.

## Positioning, in the client's own words

> A one-stop shop for dealer-quality work, SMART repairs and fitting security systems and customisation upgrades.

Founder-led business built out of mobile repairs - "numerous sleepless nights, long days, and many miles travelled." Customer satisfaction described as the number one priority since day one. This story is a genuine asset for the About page (see design doc for how to use it).

## Accreditations - unresolved, do not publish yet

No accreditation checkboxes were ticked on the form (BS 10125, Thatcham, IMI, ADAS, aluminium certified, Motor Ombudsman, Which? Trusted Trader, ISO 9001 all blank), but the client separately wrote that he holds:

- IMI accreditation for wiring and security systems
- IMI accreditation for interior repairs

No manufacturer approvals, insurer approvals, or work-provider approvals were listed - assume none exist.

**Do not publish any accreditation claim without the certificate in hand.** This is one of the biggest trust signals on a bodyshop site, which cuts both ways - a false or unverifiable claim is a real liability. Chase the certificates and logo files before the accreditations strip goes live.

## Guarantee - needs tightening before publication

Client's answer: "depending on quality of repair it can be lifetime or 6 months." Do not publish "lifetime guarantee" as a standalone headline claim - it needs to be tiered by job type (e.g. what qualifies for lifetime vs. what's covered for 6 months) before it's customer-facing. Get this in writing from the client in plain terms.

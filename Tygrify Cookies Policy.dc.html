1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work properly, remember your preferences, and let us understand how the site is used.

2. How we use cookies

We use a small number of cookies, grouped into the categories below. Strictly necessary cookies are always on because the site cannot function without them; the others are only set if you agree to them.

3. Cookies we use

CategoryPurposeDuration
Strictly necessaryKeeps you signed in to your Tygrify account and remembers items in an active quote request.Session / up to 30 days
PreferencesRemembers display choices, such as a dismissed cookie banner.Up to 12 months
AnalyticsHelps us understand which pages are visited so we can improve the site. Only set with your consent.Up to 24 months

4. Managing cookies

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies through the cookie banner when you first visit the site. You can also control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings - note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the site, such as your account, from working properly.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

6. Contact us

Questions about cookies on this site can be sent to thushiharan@tygrify.co.uk. See also our privacy policy for how we handle personal data more broadly.

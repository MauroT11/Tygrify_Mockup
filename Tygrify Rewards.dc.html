Tygrify XP

Every job earns you something back

Tygrify XP is our loyalty programme for repeat customers. You earn points automatically on every repair, valet or detail we do for you, then spend them on real rewards - no app to download and nothing to remember to claim.

How you earn

No sign-up bonus games, just points for the work you already get done

Every pound you spend with us on repairs, detailing or bodywork earns a point. There is no separate loyalty card or check-in - points are added to your account the moment a job is marked complete.

Refer a friend and you both get a head start: you get 50 points as soon as their first job is booked in.

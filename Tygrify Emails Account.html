Accounts, contact form & rewards emails

16 templates across three groups: accounts and security, the contact form, and rewards. Same 600px table layout and inline styles as the quotes set. Sample data follows Sam Carter, the customer in the account mockup.

Security emails cannot be unsubscribed from. Buttons link to mockup pages for now and become signed links in the live build. Links marked # are one-time tokens generated by the backend.

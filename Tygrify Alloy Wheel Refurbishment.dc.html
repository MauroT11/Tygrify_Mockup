Common questions

How much does alloy wheel refurbishment cost?

£100 per wheel.

How long does it take?

Same day to 3 days, depending on how many wheels and the extent of the damage.

Can you change the colour of my wheels?

Yes. We can refinish wheels in their original colour or in a different one if you want a change.

Can a cracked or buckled wheel be refurbished?

Refurbishment covers kerbing, scuffs and corrosion. If a wheel is cracked or structurally buckled, send us photos and we will tell you whether it can be repaired or needs replacing.

Home / About us

Started in a van. Same standard, bigger workshop.

Tygrify is a founder-led bodyshop on Brays Lane in Coventry. It grew out of mobile repair work, and nearly all of the work still comes in the same way it did then - somebody telling somebody else.

The story

Five years of learning it properly

The business began as mobile repairs - going out to cars wherever they were, on driveways and in car parks, fixing scuffs, dents and paint damage on the spot. Five years in the industry, and Tygrify itself trading since 2024.

Getting from there to a workshop took, in the founder's own words, "numerous sleepless nights, long days, and many miles travelled." Customer satisfaction has been the number one priority since day one, and that is not a slogan - when the only marketing you have is word of mouth, it is the whole business model.

Today the unit on Brays Lane handles bodywork, paint, cosmetic work and vehicle security in one place, so a car does not have to go to three different people to come back right.

"A one-stop shop for dealer-quality work, SMART repairs and fitting security systems and customisation upgrades."

Thushiharan, founder - Tygrify Ltd
